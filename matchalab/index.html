Welcome to The Matcha Lab

The Matcha Lab brings authentic Japanese matcha to the community through handcrafted drinks and pop-up experiences. Our goal is to make high-quality Japanese matcha approachable, enjoyable, and part of your everyday routine.

From traditional matcha lattes to creative drinks such as strawberry matcha, each drink is prepared with care and inspired by Japanese culture.
