Matcha With a Purpose

Our pop-ups are about more than serving drinks. By collaborating with student organizations, The Matcha Lab creates opportunities for students to connect while supporting organizations and activities across campus.

A portion of sales from our collaborations goes back to the participating student organization, helping support its activities and initiatives.
